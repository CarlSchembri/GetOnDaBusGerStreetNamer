PRE-BUILD DECLARATION

What content type does your game currently generate manually, inconsistently, or not at all?
Street name signs for Fairweather intersections. Right now I'm typing each one by hand
directly into the WBP_StreetSign widget, one at a time, with no consistency check.

What specific rule from your GDD must every piece of that content satisfy?
Every sign must: (1) fit the physical sign panel's character limit, (2) use a standard
abbreviated suffix (ST, AVE, BLVD, RD, LN, DR, WAY, CT, or PL) matching real-world street
sign convention, and (3) be an original Fairweather name, never a real-world street.

What does a failure look like - concretely, in your game's terms?
Text overflowing off the physical sign panel and becoming illegible, or a spelled-out
suffix like "Boulevard" instead of "BLVD" that breaks Fairweather's signage convention
and looks inconsistent next to every other sign in the town.
